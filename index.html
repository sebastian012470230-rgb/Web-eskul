The Quintessential Quintuplets

Made by Steven

Characters

Ichika Nakano

She is the eldest out of all 5 Nakano quintuplets. She is a cheerful, mature, and caring girl who often acts like an older sister to her siblings. She is a successful actress and enjoys teasing her sisters.

Nino Nakano

Nino is the second eldest out of the Nakano quintuplets. She has a strong personality and is very protective of her sisters. She can be really stubborn sometimes but she deeply cares about her family and is also very talented at cooking.

Miku Nakano

Miku is the third Nakano sister. She is quiet, shy, and somewhat introverted. She has a strong interest in Japanese history, especially historical military commanders. Despite her timid personality, she gradually becomes more confident throughout the story.

Yotsuba Nakano

Yotsuba is the fourth Nakano sister. She is energetic, cheerful, and always willing to help others. She enjoys sports and is known for her outgoing personality. She often puts other people's needs before her own.

Itsuki Nakano

Itsuki is the youngest of the Nakano quintuplets. She is hardworking, responsible, and determined to improve her studies. She loves eating and often has disagreements with Fuutarou, but she genuinely cares about her family.

Fuutarou Uesugi

Fuutarou is a hardworking and intelligent high school student who becomes the tutor of the Nakano quintuplets. He is serious about his studies and initially struggles to get along with the five sisters. However, he gradually develops a close relationship with them.
